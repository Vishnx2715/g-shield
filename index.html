The Challenge

The invisible industrial hazard

Hydrogen sulfide (H₂S) is a toxic, often odor-masking gas that can build up quickly in confined or poorly ventilated industrial settings, making it difficult for workers to gauge their own exposure in real time.

  • Refineries
  • Wastewater treatment plants
  • Sewage systems
  • Petrochemical industries
  • Confined spaces

Early Exposure

Workers may be exposed to low concentrations before the hazard becomes sensory or obvious.

Repeated Exposure

Multiple low-level exposure events across a shift are difficult to track manually without wearables.

Limited Exposure History

Conventional safety gear rarely maintains a continuous personal digital exposure record.
